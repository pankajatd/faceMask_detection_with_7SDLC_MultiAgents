## 1. Defect Description
Anatomical region-of-interest (ROI) boundary truncation error in `classify_mask_region()`. The vertical crop boundary for mask feature analysis was restricted to `fh * 0.70` to `fh * 0.98` (lower 28% of face height). This narrow bounding box evaluated only the mouth and chin, completely omitting the nose bridge and nostrils. Consequently, when subjects wore folded N95 respirators or white surgical masks pulled tightly over the nose bridge, the classifier missed the fabric boundary and falsely reported `No Mask`.

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. Ingest an image of a medical worker wearing a high-profile N95 mask covering the nose bridge down to the chin (`white_n95_mask.jpg`).
2. Observe ROI slicing in `src/detector.py`:
   ```python
   mouth_roi = face_roi[int(fh * 0.70):int(fh * 0.98), int(fw * 0.25):int(fw * 0.75)]
   ```
3. Notice that the crop begins at 70% of facial height, completely omitting the nose and sampling only the shadow fold below the lips.
4. Texture standard deviation in the shadow fold exceeded threshold `14.0`, causing the algorithm to report `No Mask`.

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Behavior
- **Expected:** The mask inspection ROI must encompass the complete mid-to-lower respiratory zone (from the bridge/tip of the nose at ~55% face height down to the lower jaw at ~92% face height).
- **Actual:** ROI started too low at 70%, ignoring nose coverage and misclassifying properly worn N95 respirators as non-compliant.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis
The developer hardcoded `0.70` to `0.98` based on generic mouth detection assumptions, failing to account for medical PPE standards where protective masks must cover the nose, mouth, and chin simultaneously.

## 5. Developer Resolution (Applied by Software Developer Agent)
- In `src/detector.py`, recalibrated the anatomical bounding box to:
  ```python
  mouth_roi = face_roi[int(fh * 0.55):int(fh * 0.92), int(fw * 0.20):int(fw * 0.80)]
  ```
- Expanded the vertical span from 28% to 37% of facial height (covering 55% to 92%), ensuring both the nose tip and mouth are evaluated.
- Expanded horizontal span from 50% to 60% of facial width (20% to 80%) to include cheek-seal edges.
