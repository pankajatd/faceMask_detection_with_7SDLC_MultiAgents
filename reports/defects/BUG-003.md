## 1. Defect Description
Feature classification logic flaw in dark fabric mask detection. The classifier evaluated dark/black masks using only a simple mean brightness threshold (`mean_brightness < 65`). When testing unmasked male subjects with dark facial hair (beards or thick mustaches), the lower facial ROI exhibited low mean luminance ($< 65$), causing the system to mistakenly classify unmasked bearded men as wearing a "Black Fabric Protective Mask".

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. Ingest an image of an unmasked person with a dark beard (`tests/test_images/diverse_tests/4_person_bare_face_male.jpg`).
2. Call `classify_mask_region(face_roi)`.
3. Check condition in `src/detector.py`:
   ```python
   is_black_mask = (mean_brightness < 65)
   ```
4. Observe that the beard pixels satisfy `mean_brightness < 65`.
5. The algorithm outputs `label = "Mask"` with confidence `0.88` on an unmasked person.

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Behavior
- **Expected:** An unmasked person with a beard must be classified as `No Mask`.
- **Actual:** The system classified unmasked bearded faces as compliant masked faces (`Mask`).

## 4. Root Cause Analysis
The developer assumed low luminance in the lower face uniquely identifies black cloth masks. However, while smooth cloth masks have uniform low-variance texture ($\sigma < 14.0$), human facial hair possesses high-frequency textural variance ($\sigma > 22.0$) due to individual hair follicles, lips, and skin contrast. Relying solely on brightness without evaluating texture variance created a critical false-positive vulnerability.

## 5. Developer Resolution (Applied by Software Developer Agent)
- In `src/detector.py`, added a multi-feature compound assertion requiring both low luminance and fabric textural smoothness:
  ```python
  # C. Dark / Black mask (Low brightness AND uniform fabric texture)
  is_black_mask = (mean_brightness < 65) and (mean_std < 14.0)
  ```
- Evaluated `std_rgb` across the lower facial ROI to ensure high-variance human beards ($\sigma > 22.0$) are rejected from black mask classification and routed to the bare-face classifier.
